An atlas made by listening

Not just what
words mean.

BOLI is a home for the expressions that rarely make it into dictionaries: ordinary phrases shaped by a place, a relationship, a moment.

Why an atlas?

Translation carries a meaning. A voice carries a world.

Everyday expressions hold more than a definition. They carry rhythm, tone, familiarity, humour, and the small details of where people live and how they meet one another. A direct translation can miss the context that makes a phrase feel like itself.

BOLI gathers phrases alongside the voices and explanations of the people who use them. It begins with Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, and Malayalam, and grows through community contributions.

Gnani AI's Prisma v3.0 can help create a first transcript from a short recording. A person reviews and corrects it; machine output is never presented as cultural truth. Every contribution is pending review, and recordings in this MVP are stored locally.
